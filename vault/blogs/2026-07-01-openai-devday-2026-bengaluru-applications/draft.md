# OpenAI DevDay 2026 Goes Global — Bengaluru Made the List, and SF Applications Close July 10

OpenAI DevDay 2026 is September 29 in San Francisco, with applications open now and closing **July 10**. For the first time, OpenAI is pairing the flagship conference with a global **DevDay Exchanges** tour — eight cities including **Bengaluru**, Tokyo, Seoul, Paris, Berlin, London, São Paulo, and Mexico City. The SF event has a $650 ticket; Bengaluru Exchange applications are not yet open. Here is exactly what each means for you.

What most coverage misses: prior DevDays (2023 through 2025) were invitation-only and free. The 2026 shift to a $650 paid application is OpenAI's first deliberate move to treat developer community access as a revenue line rather than a marketing cost. At 1,500+ expected attendees, the SF event generates nearly $1 million in ticket revenue before any sponsorship. That context matters when you're deciding whether to apply.

## What the SF Main Event Is — and Isn't

OpenAI DevDay 2026 is a single-day technical conference at **Fort Mason Center, San Francisco** on **September 29, 2026**. The format: technical deep-dives on APIs and developer tools, hands-on demos, workshops, and direct access to the OpenAI teams building the platform. It targets hands-on engineers, technical founders, and researchers actively building with AI — not executives or observers.

Key logistics:

- **Application deadline:** July 10, 2026 — [apply at devday.openai.com/apply](https://devday.openai.com/apply)
- **Acceptance timeline:** late July; you'll have 14 days to register or your spot moves to the waitlist
- **Ticket cost:** $650
- **Scholarships:** available (cover registration, travel, and accommodation for qualifying builders)
- **Can't make it?** The opening keynote is **livestreamed for free**, no sign-up required. Session recordings post to openai.com after the event.

## DevDay Exchanges: The Global Expansion Explained

The structural change for 2026 is the **DevDay Exchanges program** — OpenAI's first international developer event series. Cities on the list: **Bengaluru, Tokyo, Seoul, Paris, Berlin, London, São Paulo, and Mexico City**.

One clarification that every Indian developer needs to see before they read further: **the July 10 deadline is for the SF main event only**. The Bengaluru Exchange has no confirmed date and no open application window as of today. [Sign up at devday.openai.com](https://devday.openai.com) to get notified when Bengaluru applications open. The Exchange format is expected to differ from the SF event — likely shorter and lower-cost — but OpenAI has not published specifics yet.[^1]

[^1]: devday.openai.com, retrieved 2026-07-01: "Sign up to be the first to know when applications open" for Exchanges.

## Is the $650 SF Ticket Worth It?

Worth applying for the SF event if:
- You're actively shipping commercial products on OpenAI APIs (Realtime, Assistants, Structured Outputs) and need early signal on what's coming in Q4 2026
- You can justify San Francisco travel — the event is one day, so co-locate with other meetings if you can
- You want the highest-density access to OpenAI's API product teams that exists anywhere in the year

Skip SF if:
- You're in Bengaluru and primarily interested in the Exchange — wait for the local event, which will likely be significantly cheaper
- You're still evaluating which AI provider to build on; the livestream and posted recordings give you the content without the $650
- Your team is pre-revenue and the scholarship criteria aren't a fit

## What the Monetization Shift Actually Signals

Previous DevDays were goodwill investments: OpenAI recruited engineers and rewarded power users with access. Paid tickets change that frame. This is not a criticism — it reflects that OpenAI's developer platform is now a core revenue product, and the event budget is structured accordingly.

For builders planning annual conference spend, this means DevDay now competes line-item with events like AWS re:Invent and Google Next, not with free developer meetups. Factor that into your Q3/Q4 travel budget.

2. **If you can justify SF travel**, apply by July 10 at [devday.openai.com/apply](https://devday.openai.com/apply); applicants hear back late July
3. **Watch the free keynote livestream** on September 29 regardless — tooling announcements from DevDay consistently shape the OpenAI API roadmap for the following two quarters
