# How to List Certifications on Your Resume in 2026 So ATS and Recruiters See Them

List certifications on your resume in a dedicated Certifications section, using the official credential name, issuer, exam code when relevant, issue date, expiry or valid-through date, and credential URL. Put a required or role-defining certification in your headline or summary too, so both ATS parsing fields and human skims catch it.

The contrarian point: the real risk is not the viral "75% of resumes are rejected by ATS" story. It is more ordinary and more fixable. Your certification has to survive a database parser that may miss credentials and a person who is scanning for exact role evidence.

![Laptop resume screen with certification badges and a structured applicant tracking checklist](/img/blogs/how-to-list-certifications-on-resume/hero.png)

## Put certifications where both systems expect them

Use a dedicated section named **Certifications** or **Certifications and Licenses**. Keep it plain text, one credential per bullet, and place it above Education when certifications are more relevant than your degree for the target job.

This is not resume folklore. Workday explains that applicant tracking systems parse resumes into structured profiles with fields such as education, skills, and work history, and recruiters can search profiles using filters such as required certifications ([Workday, retrieved 2026-07-28](https://www.workday.com/en-us/topics/hr/applicant-tracking-system.html)). SHRM's summary of SHRM Foundation data is the stronger warning: only about one-third of organizations using an ATS said the system recognizes skilled credentials or certifications, while 45% of employees hold skilled credentials ([SHRM, retrieved 2026-07-28](https://www.shrm.org/executive-network/insights/breaking-paper-ceiling-skills-credentials-not-degrees-future-hiring)).

That gap puts the burden on the resume. A buried sentence like "trained in AWS and Security+" is easy to skim past and easy to parse inconsistently. A clean section heading gives both readers a predictable place to look.

## Write the credential as a database record, not a slogan

Each certification entry should answer six questions: what is the official name, who issued it, which version or exam code applies, when did you earn it, when is it valid through, and where can the employer verify it?

Use this format:

```text
CompTIA Security+ ce (SY0-701) | CompTIA
Issued: Mar 2026 | Valid through: Mar 2029
Credential ID: ABCD-1234 | Verify: https://www.credly.com/...

AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate | Amazon Web Services
Issued: Jun 2025 | Valid through: Jun 2028
Verify: https://www.credly.com/...
```

The version details matter because certifications age. AWS says its certifications are valid for three years from the date earned and must be recertified to stay current ([AWS, retrieved 2026-07-28](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/recertification/)). CompTIA's renewal policy also uses a three-year renewal cycle for continuing education certifications ([CompTIA, retrieved 2026-07-28](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/resources/test-policies/continuing-education-policies/certification-renewal-policy/)).

If you are deciding which AWS credential deserves resume space, use the analysis in [Is AWS Certification Worth It in 2026?](/blog/2026-07-27-is-aws-certification-worth-it-2026?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=career-seo-w36&utm_content=how-to-list-certifications-on-resume). If the certification maps to specific roles, cross-check it against [What Jobs Can I Get With AWS Certification?](/blog/2026-07-27-what-jobs-can-i-get-with-aws-certification?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=career-seo-w36&utm_content=how-to-list-certifications-on-resume).

## Feature only the certifications that support the target job

List the certifications that prove a required or differentiating skill for the role. Do not dump every course, webinar, and badge into the same section.

The reason is search intent and recruiter attention. NACE's Job Outlook 2026 coverage says 70% of employers report using skills-based hiring, with the approach used during screening by 65% and interviewing by 87%; GPA screening fell from 73% in 2019 to 42% in the same NACE comparison ([NACE, retrieved 2026-07-28](https://www.naceweb.org/job-market/trends-and-predictions/employer-use-of-skills-based-hiring-practices-grows)). Certifications are useful when they function as verifiable skill evidence for the job in front of you.

For a data analyst resume, feature credentials tied to SQL, analytics tools, Python, cloud data, or visualization before general productivity certificates. Use [Best Certifications for Data Analyst Roles](/blog/2026-07-25-best-certifications-for-data-analyst?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=career-seo-w36&utm_content=how-to-list-certifications-on-resume) to choose the short list. For cybersecurity, entry-level certifications can matter more when experience is thin: ISC2 surveyed 929 hiring managers and found that 89% would consider entry- and junior-level candidates holding only an entry-level cybersecurity certification, while 84% use skills-based assessments for those applicants ([ISC2, retrieved 2026-07-28](https://www.isc2.org/Insights/2025/06/cybersecurity-hiring-trends-study)). Fortinet's 2025 skills-gap survey, covering more than 1,850 IT and cybersecurity decision-makers across 29 countries, separately found that 89% prefer to hire candidates with certifications ([Fortinet, retrieved 2026-07-28](https://www.fortinet.com/corporate/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/2025/fortinet-annual-report-indicates-ai-skillsets-critical-to-cybersecurity-skills-gap-solution)).

## Put marquee certifications in the headline or summary too

If a certification is named in the job description or defines your target role, repeat it once in your headline or summary, then list the full record in Certifications.

Example:

```text
Headline:
Junior Cybersecurity Analyst | CompTIA Security+ ce | SOC Monitoring Projects

Certifications:
CompTIA Security+ ce (SY0-701) | CompTIA
Issued: Mar 2026 | Valid through: Mar 2029 | Credential URL
```

This is not keyword stuffing; it is placement discipline. HR Dive's coverage of the Ladders 2018 eye-tracking study reported an average 7.4-second initial resume skim, with a caveat that the study was vendor-published and not a broad academic benchmark ([HR Dive, retrieved 2026-07-28](https://www.hrdive.com/news/eye-tracking-study-shows-recruiters-look-at-resumes-for-7-seconds/541582/)). Use that finding lightly: the practical lesson is clear section titles and visible proof, not panic about a universal stopwatch.

The same logic applies after screening. ISC2 found 84% of hiring managers use skills-based assessments for entry- and junior-level cybersecurity applicants, so your resume should make the credential visible and your prep should prove the skill. If AWS is part of your target path, pair the resume edit with [AWS Interview Questions for Freshers](/blog/2026-07-25-aws-interview-questions-for-freshers?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=career-seo-w36&utm_content=how-to-list-certifications-on-resume).

## Handle in-progress, expired, and online certificates honestly

List in-progress certifications only when you have an exam date or a credible completion window. Mark them as "Expected" and do not imply you already hold the credential.

Use this format:

```text
AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner | Amazon Web Services
Expected: Sep 2026 | Exam scheduled
```

Expired certifications can stay if they explain older experience, but label them clearly:

```text
AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate | Amazon Web Services
Issued: Jun 2021 | Expired: Jun 2024
Used in: migration support project, 2021-2023
```

Short online course certificates belong only when they map directly to the role and the issuer is credible. A certificate of completion is not the same thing as a proctored industry certification; mixing them weakens the section. Pearson VUE's 2025 report, based on nearly 24,000 IT professionals, shows certification momentum is still real: 82% of respondents said earning a certification gave them confidence to explore or pursue new job opportunities, and AI/ML certification plans had more than doubled from 17% in 2022 to 35% in 2024 ([Pearson VUE, retrieved 2026-07-28](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/about/news/highlights/certifications-fuel-the-success-in-the-age-of-ai.html)). Use that momentum selectively, not as an excuse to list weak evidence.

## Retire the ATS myths and fix the real failure modes

Do not write for a mythical robot that rejects 75% of resumes. Write for the real system: software that parses, filters, ranks, and stores candidate data differently by employer configuration.

Harvard Business School's Hidden Workers project says hiring processes increasingly seek "perfect" candidates efficiently and can systematically exclude qualified workers ([HBS, retrieved 2026-07-28](https://www.hbs.edu/managing-the-future-of-work/research/hidden-workers-untapped-talent)). The synthesis for this post uses the HBS/Accenture report as the real ATS-filtering evidence: more than 90% of employers use recruiting-management systems to initially filter or rank candidates, and 88% of employers agree qualified high-skills candidates can be screened out for not matching exact criteria.

Greenhouse's candidate FAQ is also a useful guardrail: the candidate profile can store resume and profile data, but Greenhouse cannot change an application or make hiring decisions for the employer ([Greenhouse, retrieved 2026-07-28](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/43418495049499-MyGreenhouse-FAQ-for-Candidates)). That is why the fix is boring and powerful: use a plain section, official names, dates, credential URLs, and job-description alignment.

For entry-level tech candidates, this precision matters because there are fewer easy shots. Indeed Hiring Lab reported that in Q2 2025 only 18% of U.S. tech postings that stated experience requirements were open to candidates with one year or less of relevant experience ([Indeed Hiring Lab, retrieved 2026-07-28](https://www.hiringlab.org/2025/07/30/experience-requirements-have-tightened-amid-the-tech-hiring-freeze/)). If cybersecurity is your switch path, connect the resume section to a broader plan with [How to Get Into Cybersecurity With No Experience](/blog/2026-07-14-how-to-get-into-cybersecurity-with-no-experience?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=career-seo-w36&utm_content=how-to-list-certifications-on-resume).
