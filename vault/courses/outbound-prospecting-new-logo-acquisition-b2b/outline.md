## Chapter 1: Building an ICP and Target Account List for Corporate Learning Buyers
**Duration:** ~12 min | **Dossier:** vault/research/courses/outbound-prospecting-new-logo-acquisition-b2b/01-building-icp-target-account-list.md

### Learning Objectives
- Build a tiered Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) using firmographic criteria — company size, industry vertical, L&D budget signals, and tech-stack indicators — relevant to corporate training buyers
- Assemble a scored target account list of at least 20 net-new accounts using a free or trial-tier prospecting tool (e.g., Apollo.io or LinkedIn Sales Navigator)
- Classify accounts into Tier 1 (dream accounts), Tier 2 (strong fit), and Tier 3 (broad fit) and document the rationale for each tier
- Identify two to three buying-committee personas per account (e.g., L&D Manager, IT Director, CLO) and map their business pain to your training portfolio

### Key Concepts
- Firmographic ICP criteria: company size, industry vertical, L&D budget signals, tech-stack indicators
- Account tiering: T1 dream, T2 strong fit, T3 broad fit — with documented rationale
- Buying-committee persona types in corporate learning: L&D Manager, IT Director, CLO
- Free/trial prospecting tools: Apollo.io, LinkedIn Sales Navigator

### Hands-On Exercise
Build a 20-account target list in Apollo.io or LinkedIn Sales Navigator, score and tier every account, and map two personas per Tier 1 account to a specific business pain.

## Chapter 2: Reading and Acting on Buying Signals Before Your Competitor Does
**Duration:** ~10 min | **Dossier:** vault/research/courses/outbound-prospecting-new-logo-acquisition-b2b/02-reading-acting-buying-signals.md

### Learning Objectives
- Configure at least two signal-monitoring sources (e.g., LinkedIn job-change alerts, Google Alerts for target accounts, intent data in a free Apollo or HubSpot trial) to surface trigger events for Tier 1 accounts
- Map three specific trigger types to outreach-worthy moments for corporate training buyers (e.g., 10+ IT role postings signal a certification need)
- Prioritize a 10-account shortlist from your Tier 1 list by combining ICP fit score with recency of trigger event
- Distinguish activity-based metrics (emails sent) from outcome-based metrics (positive reply rate) and set a weekly tracking baseline

### Key Concepts
- Trigger event types: new hires, budget cycles, compliance deadlines, headcount surges
- Signal-monitoring tools: LinkedIn Alerts, Google Alerts, Apollo/HubSpot intent data
- Signal-to-outreach mapping for corporate training context
- Activity vs. outcome metrics — establishing a baseline

### Hands-On Exercise
Configure two monitoring sources for your Tier 1 accounts, log three trigger events, and rank a 10-account shortlist by ICP fit + signal recency.

## Chapter 3: Crafting Signal-Triggered Outreach Sequences Across Email and LinkedIn
**Duration:** ~15 min | **Dossier:** vault/research/courses/outbound-prospecting-new-logo-acquisition-b2b/03-crafting-signal-triggered-outreach-sequences.md

### Learning Objectives
- Write a three-message email sequence for one Tier 1 account: a signal-triggered first touch referencing a specific event, a value-add follow-up anchored in an industry stat or case study, and a breakup message that closes the loop cleanly
- Draft a LinkedIn connection request and follow-up InMail for the same account that references the prospect's content or role activity rather than leading with a product pitch
- Sequence the combined email-and-LinkedIn touches into a 10–12 touch cadence spread across 4–6 weeks using a free sequencing tool or manually managed spreadsheet
- Apply tiered personalization: full custom research for Tier 1, persona-level templates for Tier 2, and ICP-fit templates for Tier 3

### Key Concepts
- Signal-triggered email sequence structure: first touch, value-add, breakup
- LinkedIn outreach: role-referenced connection requests vs. product-led InMails
- Cadence math: 10–12 touches, 4–6 weeks, channel mix
- Tiered personalization depth by account tier

### Hands-On Exercise
Write a complete 3-message email sequence + LinkedIn connection request + follow-up InMail for one Tier 1 account, then map all 10–12 touches on a cadence calendar.

## Chapter 4: Generating Referral Pipeline from Existing Client Networks
**Duration:** ~12 min | **Dossier:** vault/research/courses/outbound-prospecting-new-logo-acquisition-b2b/04-generating-referral-pipeline.md

### Learning Objectives
- Identify at least five existing client contacts who have the network, credibility, and motivation to refer you to a peer organization in the same industry vertical
- Construct a referral ask script that ties the introduction request to a concrete outcome the existing client has already experienced
- Build a simple referral tracking log in your CRM (or a spreadsheet) that records ask date, referral status, follow-up date, and outcome
- Diagnose two reasons a referral ask fails — wrong timing, wrong contact, vague ask, no reciprocal value — and rewrite a failed ask to address the root cause

### Key Concepts
- Referral contact qualification: network breadth, credibility in the vertical, motivation to refer
- Outcome-anchored referral ask scripts (vs. generic asks)
- Referral tracking: CRM fields and spreadsheet structure
- Root-cause diagnosis for failed referral asks with rewrite framework

### Hands-On Exercise
Identify five referral candidates from your existing accounts, write one outcome-anchored referral ask script, build your referral tracking log, and diagnose a past failed ask.

## Chapter 5: Running a Full New-Logo Pursuit — From First Signal to First Meeting
**Duration:** ~15 min | **Dossier:** vault/research/courses/outbound-prospecting-new-logo-acquisition-b2b/05-running-full-new-logo-pursuit.md

### Learning Objectives
- Select one net-new target account and document the ICP fit score, the specific trigger event that prompted outreach, and the two-persona buying committee you will pursue
- Execute a minimum of five sequenced touches across email and LinkedIn over two weeks, logging each touch and the prospect's response in your CRM or tracking sheet
- Evaluate the sequence performance against your positive-reply-rate baseline and identify one specific message or timing change that would improve results, supported by the data
- Prepare a one-page new-logo pipeline report showing accounts contacted, touches sent, meetings booked or pending, and the next action for each account

### Key Concepts
- End-to-end prospecting motion: ICP → signal → sequence → referral integration
- Live execution logging: touch types, dates, responses, CRM fields
- Performance evaluation: positive-reply rate vs. baseline, data-driven iteration
- Pipeline report structure for weekly manager review

### Hands-On Exercise
Run a live 2-week prospecting sprint on one net-new account, log all touches, evaluate performance against your baseline, and deliver a one-page pipeline report.

## Capstone
Learners complete a full new-logo prospecting sprint: build a 20-account target list, configure signal monitoring, write and execute a 10–12 touch multi-channel sequence, make at least one referral ask, and deliver a pipeline report showing meetings booked and next actions — the exact deliverable an account manager presents in a weekly pipeline review.
