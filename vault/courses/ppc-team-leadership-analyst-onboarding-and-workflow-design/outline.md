## Chapter 1: Designing a 3-4 Person PPC Analyst Team for a Data-Driven Analytics Firm

**Duration:** ~12 min
**Learning objectives:**
- Build a 3-4 person PPC analyst org chart with one primary owner for tracking, analysis, media buying, and reporting
- Map paid media strategist, analytics engineer, tracking specialist, and campaign analyst seats to daily PPC workflows
- Assign CPA, ROAS, CTR, and conversion-rate accountability without duplicate ownership
- Decide which analyst decisions are autonomous and which require PPC manager sign-off

**Key concepts:** role architecture, ownership boundaries, PPC team RACI, KPI accountability, decision rights, manager sign-off rules

**Hands-on exercise:** Given two candidate team configurations and a campaign-volume scenario, select the stronger structure and produce a one-page role matrix that maps each analyst seat to workflow ownership, KPI accountability, and sign-off boundaries.

## Chapter 2: Writing Analyst Job Descriptions and Running a Skills-Based Hiring Process

**Duration:** ~13 min
**Learning objectives:**
- Write a PPC analyst job description around measurable outcomes rather than activity lists
- Specify the required platform proficiencies for Google Ads, GA4, and Looker Studio in 2026
- Build a five-question skills-based interview scorecard with pass/fail grading criteria
- Design and evaluate a 30-minute take-home PPC account audit task

**Key concepts:** outcome-based job descriptions, structured interviews, pass/fail scorecards, take-home audits, root-cause diagnosis, candidate ranking

**Hands-on exercise:** Draft a PPC analyst job description, create a five-question interview scorecard, then evaluate three sample candidate responses to an anonymised underperforming-account audit and rank them with written justification.

## Chapter 3: Onboarding Analysts with a 30-60-90 Day Ramp Plan Tied to Campaign Ownership

**Duration:** ~12 min
**Learning objectives:**
- Draft a 30-60-90 day ramp plan with deliverables, platform tasks, and access grants at each milestone
- Build a day-one access checklist for Google Ads, GA4, CRM connectors, and Looker Studio
- Define measurable onboarding KPIs such as time-to-first-solo-campaign, QA pass rate, and report accuracy score
- Diagnose a day-45 underperformance scenario and prescribe targeted interventions

**Key concepts:** onboarding milestones, access readiness, campaign QA, first optimisation recommendation, ramp KPIs, supervised-to-independent ownership

**Hands-on exercise:** Create a 30-60-90 onboarding plan for one analyst role, including access checklist, milestone deliverables, KPI thresholds, and two interventions for an analyst who is behind target at day 45.

---

## Chapter 4: Distributing Campaign Portfolios and Setting Weekly Performance Accountability Rhythms

**Duration:** ~11 min
**Learning objectives:**
- Allocate a sample portfolio of eight campaigns across three analysts using spend, complexity, and conversion volume
- Design a weekly operating rhythm with stand-ups, async reporting artefacts, and escalation triggers
- Build a campaign-owner RACI for a cross-platform launch across Google Ads Search, Performance Max, and Meta Advantage+
- Define dashboard rows and rolled-up KPIs that make analyst performance gaps visible quickly

**Key concepts:** portfolio allocation, weighted scoring, budget pacing escalation, weekly operating cadence, campaign-owner RACI, analyst-level dashboards

**Hands-on exercise:** Use a weighted scoring model to assign eight campaigns to three analysts, document the rationale, then draft the weekly team rhythm and escalation thresholds that keep the portfolio accountable without micromanagement.

## Chapter 5: Coaching Analysts Through Performance Gaps and Running Structured 1-on-1s

**Duration:** ~10 min
**Learning objectives:**
- Run a data-grounded analyst 1-on-1 using campaign evidence rather than impressions or vague feedback
- Diagnose whether a performance gap is caused by skill, tool access, or workload distribution
- Turn vague feedback into specific, observable, time-bound improvement requests
- Design a quarterly analyst performance review scorecard that separates KPI outcomes, analysis quality, and process adherence

**Key concepts:** coaching briefs, 1-on-1 structure, performance-gap diagnosis, measurable improvement targets, feedback specificity, quarterly review scorecards

**Hands-on exercise:** Given an analyst performance dataset with declining CTR and rising CPA, write a coaching brief that identifies the likely root cause, sets a two-week measurable target, and converts one vague feedback statement into a concrete improvement request.

## Chapter 6: Scaling the Team's Output with Reporting Automation and AI-Assisted Workflows

**Duration:** ~12 min
**Learning objectives:**
- Configure a Looker Studio dashboard connected to Google Ads and GA4 that separates modelled and measured conversions
- Build an automated weekly report template for ROAS, CPA, CTR, CVR, impression share, blended CAC, and incrementality estimate
- Set anomaly alerts for budget pacing deviation and conversion tracking drops
- Correct a cross-platform report that conflates prospecting and retargeting ROAS

**Key concepts:** Looker Studio connectors, cookieless attribution, modelled conversions, measured conversions, automated weekly reporting, anomaly alerts, AI-assisted workflow triage, prospecting versus retargeting segmentation

**Hands-on exercise:** Design a reporting automation package: a Looker Studio dashboard layout, a weekly report template, two anomaly alerts, and a corrected recommendation memo for a report that mixes prospecting and retargeting ROAS.

## Capstone: PPC Analyst Team Operating System

Learners assemble a hiring-to-performance operating system for a 3-4 person PPC analyst team at a data-driven analytics firm. The deliverable includes: a role matrix from chapter 1; a job description, interview scorecard, and take-home audit from chapter 2; a 30-60-90 onboarding plan from chapter 3; campaign portfolio allocation and weekly accountability cadence from chapter 4; a coaching brief and quarterly review scorecard from chapter 5; and a reporting automation plan with anomaly alerts from chapter 6.

Verification criteria:
- Every analyst seat has exactly one primary ownership area and one clear KPI accountability set
- Hiring materials test measurable performance outcomes, not generic platform familiarity
- Onboarding milestones include access grants, campaign tasks, and measurable ramp KPIs
- Portfolio allocation uses spend, complexity, and conversion volume rather than equal distribution
- Coaching guidance uses campaign data to set a specific two-week improvement target
- Reporting automation separates measured from modelled conversions and prospecting from retargeting ROAS
