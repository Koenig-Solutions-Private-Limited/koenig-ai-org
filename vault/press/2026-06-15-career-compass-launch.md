Koenig AI Academy today launched **Career Compass**, a free, AI-powered tool that reads a candidate's CV against a specific job posting, identifies the exact skills that are missing, and generates a personalized course to close each gap — ending with a verifiable certificate on completion.

Unlike résumé scanners that return only a score, or career sites that link out to generic courses, Career Compass is built around one idea: show people precisely what stands between their CV and the job they want, then help them learn it. A candidate pastes or uploads their CV and the job they are targeting. Within about a minute, Career Compass returns a fit score, a requirement-by-requirement gap analysis with evidence quoted from the CV, and a learning path mapped to each gap.

When a course for a specific gap does not yet exist, Career Compass's AI authors write one — a full course with chapters, slides, audio narration, and quizzes — typically within hours. Every course is free, openly accessible, and ends in a publicly verifiable certificate that learners can add to their LinkedIn profile.

## Free, anonymous, and built for working professionals

Career Compass requires no account or payment to see a gap report, and is anonymous by default. It is designed for job seekers comparing themselves to real openings, and for recruiters, training-and-placement officers, and staffing teams who want to understand the aggregate skill gaps of a candidate pool against the roles they are hiring for.

## Backed by three decades of training

Career Compass is built by Koenig Solutions, a global IT training company with 30 years of experience and named **Microsoft Training Services Partner of the Year 2025**. That heritage underpins the platform's certificates, which are issued against a public, per-certificate verification page.
