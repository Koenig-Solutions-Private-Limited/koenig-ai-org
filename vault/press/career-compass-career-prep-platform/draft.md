FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

**Koenig AI Academy Launches Career Compass: One Platform to Identify Skill Gaps, Learn for Free, and Ace the Interview**

*The end-to-end career-prep platform combines an AI-powered CV-vs-JD gap analysis engine, a free personalized learning path, and an AI mock interview coach — taking job seekers from skill gap to interview-ready in a single continuous flow.*

**NEW DELHI, June 26, 2026** — Koenig AI Academy today announced the full launch of **Career Compass** (academy.koenig-solutions.com), an end-to-end career preparation platform that merges two complementary capabilities into a single, uninterrupted workflow: an AI-driven gap analysis engine that reads a candidate's CV against a real job description and builds a free personalized learning path to close every identified gap, and an AI mock interview coach — voice and chat — that prepares candidates to prove their readiness when it counts.

The platform is built around one continuous sequence: **Analyze → Learn → Practice → Interview Ready.**

---

### From Upload to a Free, Personalized Learning Plan

Career Compass begins the moment a job seeker uploads their CV and pastes a target job description. The gap analysis engine compares the two documents and surfaces a precise, ranked list of missing or underdeveloped skills — the exact delta between the candidate's current profile and the role's requirements.

It then generates a free, personalized learning path drawn from the Koenig AI Academy course catalog, with each recommended module mapped directly to a named gap. Candidates see not just what to learn, but why — and in which order.

"Most job seekers either overestimate their readiness or have no reliable way to know what to prioritize," said Vardaan Koenig, Founder of Koenig AI Academy. "Career Compass turns 'I need to upskill' into a specific, actionable plan — free of charge. No guesswork, no one-size-fits-all curriculum."

The Academy's career catalog spans 6+ courses across technical, marketing-technology, infrastructure, and business disciplines, with new courses added weekly.

---

### Practice the Interview Before It Counts

Once candidates have worked through their personalized learning path, Career Compass's AI interview coach steps in. The mock interview engine simulates a realistic interviewer: it asks role-relevant questions mapped to the candidate's specific gaps, evaluates responses for content, delivery, and clarity, and delivers specific, actionable feedback after each answer — not just a score. Sessions run in voice (spoken exchange with real-time transcript) or chat, according to the candidate's preference.

Candidates can retry answers, run multiple rounds, or focus on a single question until they are confident. No recording is stored beyond the session, consistent with Career Compass's anonymous-by-default approach.

---

### One Platform, End to End

"The most common mistake in career preparation is treating skill gaps and interview readiness as two separate problems," Koenig added. "Career Compass treats them as one continuous challenge. Analyze the gap, close it with a free course and verifiable certificate, then practise until you can articulate it under pressure — all in the same platform."

Career Compass is free to use, requires no account, and is anonymous by default.
