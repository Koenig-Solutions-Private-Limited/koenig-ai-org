Koenig AI Academy today detailed a capability at the core of its Career Compass platform: when a learner identifies a skill gap that no existing course covers, the platform's AI content authors generate a complete, personalized course for that exact gap — and notify the learner when it is live.

Each generated course is multimodal by design: written chapters, presentation slides, audio narration, and quizzes, ending in a verifiable certificate. The academy has already produced multiple courses end-to-end through this pipeline, with a typical time from request to a live, learnable course measured in hours rather than the weeks or months a traditional curriculum requires.

## From a job posting to a course, automatically

The capability is wired directly into Career Compass's gap analysis. A candidate compares their CV to a target role; each missing requirement becomes a learning objective; and where the catalog has no match, a course is authored to fill it. The result is a learning path assembled around the individual — not a generic catalog the learner must navigate alone.

Every course remains free and openly accessible, with no account or payment required to read any chapter. Courses are authored against Koenig's editorial standards and reviewed before publication.

## Why it matters

Conventional online learning asks people to find the right course among thousands and hope it maps to the job they want. Career Compass inverts that: it starts from a specific opening, proves where the candidate falls short with evidence from their own CV, and produces the exact material to close each gap. It is a direct expression of Koenig's 30-year training heritage — Koenig Solutions was named Microsoft Training Services Partner of the Year 2025 — applied to job seekers at no cost.

## About Koenig AI Academy

Koenig AI Academy (academy.kspl.tech) is a free, anonymous-by-default learning platform by Koenig Solutions, Microsoft Training Services Partner of the Year 2025.
