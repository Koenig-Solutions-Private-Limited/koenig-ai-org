Gemini models are used in consumer products, developer APIs, and enterprise workflows. Their distinguishing design center is multimodality: many use cases combine text with images, video, audio, code, or documents.

When comparing Gemini with other model families, teams should test the exact modality mix they need. A model can be strong in visual reasoning or long-context processing while still requiring careful evaluation for structured output, determinism, latency, or tool-call reliability.
