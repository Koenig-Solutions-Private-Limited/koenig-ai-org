# Attorney-Client Privilege

In the context of AI and legal tech, **Attorney-Client Privilege** refers to the legal requirement that communications between an attorney and their client be kept confidential. When using LLMs, this requires that data processed by the model is not used for training or exposed in unsecured logs, maintaining the "circle of confidentiality" required by law.
