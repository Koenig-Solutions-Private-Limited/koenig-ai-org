## Definition
Few-shot prompting is a technique where the prompt includes a small number of concrete examples (the "shots") of input-output pairs. This guides the language model to understand the expected format, tone, or reasoning process for a new task. Unlike zero-shot prompting, which asks the model to perform a task without examples, few-shot prompting significantly improves performance on structured or complex tasks where instruction alone may be insufficient.
