Claude is used both as a chat assistant and as a model inside applications. Teams choose among Claude models based on capability, latency, context needs, cost, and the reliability requirements of the workflow.

In agent systems, Claude often acts as the planner or executor that reads instructions, calls tools, updates state, and hands work to another reviewer or automation step. The surrounding harness matters as much as the model: permissions, memory, evals, and recovery behavior determine whether the workflow is production-ready.
