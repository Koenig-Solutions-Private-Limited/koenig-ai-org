# Trusted Contacts — CMO autonomous email list

People the CMO (Chief Marketing/SEO) may **email autonomously** (no per-email approval) when a task calls for it. Anyone **not** on this list is surfaced to Rohit for approval before any email is sent.

The CMO emails a contact by posting an issue comment:
`SEND-EMAIL: to=<email-or-name>; subject=<subject>; body=<body>`
— the bridge sends it from the CMO's official address and routes the reply back into the originating issue.

## How to add a contact
One row per person. **Email is required.** Include their **designation** and the **tasks/areas they own** so the CMO emails the right person for the right thing.

| Name | Email | Designation | Tasks / areas they own |
|---|---|---|---|
| _e.g. Jane Doe_ | _jane@koenig-solutions.com_ | _Head of Content_ | _blog approvals, editorial calendar, brand voice_ |
| | | | |
