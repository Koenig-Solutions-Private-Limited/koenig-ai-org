# Message House — Career Compass by Koenig

This file is the source of truth for ALL public-facing content about Career Compass and the Academy: blog posts, social posts, newsletters, emails, PR pitches, directory listings, community answers. Every content-producing agent must align with it. Editor in Chief gates against it at G3. Proof-point numbers are refreshed monthly by the Growth Lead from live metrics.

## Roof (core value proposition)

> **Career Compass shows you exactly what stands between your CV and the job you want — then closes the gap with a free personalized course and a verifiable certificate, in days not months.**

## Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Precision
**Belief:** "It analyzes MY CV against a REAL job posting — not generic career advice."
**Proof:** the mechanism itself (requirement-by-requirement verdicts with CV evidence quoted); a sample gap report; courses generated from the candidate's actual gaps, not a catalog.

### Pillar 2 — Speed-to-credential
**Belief:** "Gap report in 2 minutes; personalized course with video, audio and quizzes in hours; verifiable certificate when you pass — free forever."
**Proof (refresh monthly):** N gap reports generated · N courses generated · N certificates issued · median click→course-live time (~1.5–4h).

### Pillar 3 — Trust
**Belief:** "Backed by Koenig Solutions — 30 years of IT training, Microsoft Training Services Partner of the Year 2025."
**Proof:** the award; public per-certificate verification pages; Koenig's vendor-authorized training heritage; named employers/recruiters who recognize the certificate (build this list).

## Differentiation line

> *Unlike LinkedIn Learning or generic MOOCs, Career Compass starts from a specific job posting and proves the gap was closed.*

Competitive frame (use when relevant, never disparage by name in public posts): resume scanners stop at a score; gap-analysis tools end in third-party course links; generic free-certificate sites teach everyone the same thing; expensive bootcamps charge lakhs against placement promises. We generate the exact course and certify completion, free.

## Persona overlays

### Job-seeker (lead with Pillar 1, mechanism-first)
- Tone: respectful coach, zero hype, zero fear-mongering.
- Hook: "Paste the job posting. Upload your CV. See your real gaps in 2 minutes — free, no signup to see your score."
- Objection — "Is an AI certificate worth anything?" → reframe: it's verifiable skills evidence on a public URL, issued by a 30-year training company; certifications correlate with raises/promotions (Pearson VUE 2025: 32% report raises post-cert).

### Recruiter / TPO / employer (lead with Pillar 3, outcomes-first)
- Hook: "See your batch's aggregate skill gaps against the jobs they actually want. Verify any candidate's certificate in one click. Zero cost to your placement cell."
- Staffing-firm angle: send rejected candidates a gap report instead of a rejection; redeploy bench consultants against the next requirement in days, with proof.

## Banned claims (hard rules)

- NO placement-rate or job-guarantee claims, ever.
- NO salary-uplift promises.
- NO disparaging named competitors in public posts.
- NO fabricated numbers — proof points come only from this file's monthly-refreshed metrics block.
- NO "free certificate 🔥" spam framing — the personalized gap report leads, the certificate follows.
