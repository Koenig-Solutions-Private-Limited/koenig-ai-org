# Career Compass × Naukri (InfoEdge) — Strategy & Call Brief

**Prepared for:** Vardaan Aggarwal · Koenig AI Academy
**Date:** 17 Jun 2026
**Product:** **Career Compass** — academy.koenig-solutions.com

---

## 1. The product, in one line

Career Compass is a free AI tool for job-seekers: **upload your CV + a target job → it analyses your skill gaps, recommends short AI/tech upskilling courses, and prepares you for the interview.** Free today; a paid tier (₹99–₹199 per course / per CV-match report) comes later. **The goal of this exercise is the *right customers and real value delivered* — not near-term revenue.**

## 2. Why Naukri's audience is the ideal fit

A Naukri job-seeker is *exactly* our customer: they already have a **CV** and an **active job target** — the two inputs Career Compass needs. We help them close their skill gap and walk into the interview prepared. Naukri has **70M+ registered job-seekers** and is India's #1 job portal, so the relevant-audience density is unmatched.

## 3. What we learned about Naukri / InfoEdge (so we go in informed)

**a) Naukri's ad products are built for recruiters, not for promoting an external site.**
Their advertising (display/branding, NVite email-to-candidates, Insta-Hire SMS, app push) is designed for *employers reaching candidates about jobs*. There is **no off-the-shelf B2C product** for sending an external upskilling site to their users — so this is the **first thing to clarify on the call** (is it allowed, and via which surface?).

**b) There is NO advertiser API / self-serve / automation — it is 100% sales-managed.**
Naukri offers job-posting APIs for recruiters (ATS integrations), but **nothing for programmatically creating/optimising ad campaigns.** Every campaign runs through an account manager. → Naukri will be a **manual, relationship-driven** channel, *not* something we can put on auto-pilot (unlike Meta/Google — see §5).

**c) Coopetition flag — InfoEdge has its own upskilling product.**
**Naukri FastForward / Naukri Learning** is InfoEdge's in-house reskilling offering. We are, in a small way, adjacent to it. Worth being aware of — it can be a *risk* (they may not want to promote a competitor) **or** an *opportunity* (a content/affiliate partnership).

**d) The best-fit InfoEdge surfaces may not be Naukri.com itself:**
- **Shiksha.com** — InfoEdge's **course-discovery** portal. Natural home for a course/upskilling offer; intent-aligned audience.
- **hirist.tech / iimjobs.com** — premium **tech** and management job audiences; high-quality, high-intent, smaller.

**e) Budget reality:** ₹1L is roughly an entry-level *managed* spend. There is no public rate card — pricing is negotiated. Expect them to propose a display + email/SMS bundle.

## 4. The 6 questions to ask Naukri on the call

1. **Policy:** Can we promote an **external upskilling site** (Career Compass) to your job-seekers — via display, email (NVite), or SMS — or are you limited to job/employer advertising only?
2. **Automation/integration:** Is there **any** API, bulk-upload, or file export/import to create and manage campaigns — or is everything via an account manager?
3. **Targeting:** Can we target by **role / skill / experience / location**? Can we upload or refine a **custom audience**?
4. **₹1L test:** For a ₹1,00,000 pilot, what product mix do you recommend, and what **CPM / CPC / cost-per-lead** should we expect? Is there a rate card?
5. **Attribution:** What **click/conversion data** do you return after a campaign, and can it feed our own analytics (UTM/postbacks)?
6. **Shiksha / partnership:** For a *course/upskilling* offer, does **Shiksha.com** — or a **FastForward content partnership** — fit better than Naukri.com display?

## 5. Where Naukri fits in the bigger picture (Naukri vs Meta)

We are testing two complementary motions:

| | **Naukri / InfoEdge** | **Meta (Facebook + Instagram)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Audience fit** | Excellent — active job-seekers with CVs | Very good — interest/lookalike targeting of upskillers |
| **Intent** | High (actively job-hunting) | Medium–high (interest-based) |
| **Automation** | **None** — fully manual/managed | **Full** — Marketing API; create→optimise→pause by software |
| **Cost (India)** | Negotiated, opaque; ₹1L = managed pilot | CPL ~**₹200–400**, CPC ~₹5–15; ₹1L ≈ **200–400 CV-analysis conversions/month** |
| **Best role** | **Relationship / managed pilot**, premium intent | **Always-on, auto-piloted volume engine** |

**Read:** Naukri is a high-intent *relationship* channel worth a managed pilot; **Meta is the channel that matches the "advertising on auto-drive" vision** — fully controllable by our AI org (create, monitor, optimise, pause) via the Marketing API. We pursue both; we *automate* Meta (and likely Google Search, the highest-intent + fully-API channel, pending a green light), and run Naukri as a managed experiment.

## 6. Recommended first move + the ₹1L framing

- **Success metric for the test = "CV-analysis completed" conversions** (a real, engaged user), *not* revenue. Our site already tracks this event (PostHog).
- **Naukri:** if the call confirms external-site promotion is allowed, run a **₹50k–₹1L managed pilot** on the best surface they recommend (likely a display + NVite email bundle, or Shiksha), with **UTM tracking** so we can measure cost-per-CV-analysis.
- **Meta (in parallel, we control):** a ₹1L test — Conversions objective optimised on `analysis_completed`, 2–3 ad sets (broad India 20–40, IT-professionals/freshers, retargeting), Reels-first creative ("Find your AI/tech skill gaps in 2 minutes — free"). Expected ~200–400 conversions/month.
- **Compare cost-per-CV-analysis across both** after 30 days → double down on the winner, automate it, and add the next channel.

## 7. What we'll need (so the test can actually run)

- **Naukri:** the call outcome (policy, targeting, pricing, attribution, Shiksha option).
- **Meta:** a Business Manager + ad account + Facebook Page + payment method (OTP/GST) + the Meta **Pixel** on the site — then we automate the rest.
- **Decision:** whether to also switch on **Google Search** (highest intent, fully automatable) — it was previously paused; the team recommends reconsidering it for this push.

---

## 7. CMO recommendation (AI marketing lead)

**Bottom line: start Meta today (automatable + measurable), use the Naukri call to open a Shiksha partnership rather than buy Naukri.com display, and keep InfoEdge spend small and attribution-gated.**

**Channel ranking:** Meta now (the only channel we control + measure in days, ~₹200–400 per CV-analysis) → Shiksha.com (course-discovery intent = our offer; cheaper/more aligned than Naukri.com display) → Naukri.com display/NVite last (recruiter-centric, opaque, manual, FastForward coopetition risk — small managed pilot only if the call clears policy + attribution). hirist/iimjobs: hold for later.

**Positioning:** don't pitch "upskilling platform" (competitor reflex vs FastForward). Pitch a **free top-of-funnel CV→skill-gap diagnostic that sends qualified, course-ready learners downstream — into their own Shiksha/FastForward courses.** That makes us an affiliate *partner* feeding intent. Lead with the Shiksha angle.

**₹1L test:** ₹60k → Meta now (Conversions on `analysis_completed`); ₹40k → held for the InfoEdge pilot, preferring a Shiksha course-intent placement, UTM-tracked. Hard gate: if Naukri can't feed click/conversion data into our UTM/PostHog, cap managed spend at ~₹25k. Single metric: **cost per completed CV-analysis**, 30-day read.

**Add to the call questions:** (a) minimum commitment / can we stop after 2 weeks? (b) exclusivity/throttling vs FastForward? (c) ask for **cost-per-qualified-click to our site** in writing (not CPM); (d) make attribution a go/no-go — can they pass UTM/click-IDs or a near-real-time postback?

**Do NOT agree to:** no minimum/multi-month lock-in; no spend without written attribution/reporting; no exclusivity blocking Meta/Google in parallel; no pure CPM branding bundle; no sharing of users' CV/PII and no NVite email/SMS blast without clear opt-in.

*The fully-automated campaign lifecycle (create→optimise→pause via the Paperclip org) is designed and ready to build for Meta/Google once ad accounts + the Pixel are live.*
